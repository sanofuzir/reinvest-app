🔌 How to connect to Tag Markets MT5 via MetaApi:
1. Create a free account at metaapi.cloud (free tier supports 1 account, real-time WebSocket streaming).
2. Add your Tag Markets MT5 account — enter broker server TagMarkets-Live, your MT5 login and password.
3. Copy your MetaApi Token and Account ID into the fields above and click Connect.
This app then connects via MetaApi's WebSocket to receive live balance, equity, and accountInformation events from your MT5 account in real time.
